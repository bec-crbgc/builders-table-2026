Meet the voices around The Builders Table

The Builders Table brings together people from across construction, training, apprenticeship support, government and industry to share perspectives, challenge thinking and strengthen the connections that shape successful careers.

Guest Speakers

People shaping the conversation

Guest Speaker

Julian Duszniak

Lead Carpenter/Supervisor | 470 Property Group Pty Ltd

Julian Duszniak is a recently qualified carpenter who completed his Certificate III in Carpentry with Builders Academy Australia and has already taken the next step in his career, progressing into a Lead Carpenter/Supervisor position with 470 Property Group Pty Ltd in South Melbourne.

Having completed his apprenticeship with the same employer he continues to work with today, Julian's journey demonstrates the career progression that can come from a successful apprenticeship — from learning the trade and developing practical capability through to taking on greater responsibility and leadership on site.

Now moving from apprentice to qualified tradesperson and emerging leader, Julian brings an important voice to The Builders Table — demonstrating where that first step into a trade can ultimately lead.

Guest Speaker

Craig Robertson

CEO | Victorian Skills Authority

Craig Robertson is CEO of the Victorian Skills Authority, which plans post-school education and training around Victoria's future skills needs and the jobs required to support a growing economy.

Previously CEO of TAFE Directors Australia, Craig also brings more than 30 years of Commonwealth and Victorian Government experience spanning employment policy, tertiary education and skills. He is a strong advocate for contemporary, future-focused vocational education and in 2024 led a tripartite working group whose recommendations for new approaches to VET qualifications were accepted by Australian skills ministers.

Guest Speaker

Kathryn Hughes

Apprenticeship Industry Leader | MEGT

Kathryn Hughes has worked across the vocational education and training sector for more than 30 years and has led MEGT's Apprentice Connect Provider field operations across Western Victoria for the past six years.

Her experience spans senior leadership, apprenticeship services, registered training organisations, employment services and national sales, giving her a broad understanding of the apprenticeship journey from both the employer and apprentice perspective.

Passionate about creating strong apprenticeship and career pathways, Kathryn is committed to mentoring her team and building lasting industry partnerships that help apprentices and employers succeed.

Guest Speaker

Ash Quiddington

Director | Oasis Building Group & Dune Building Technologies

Ash Quiddington is Director of Oasis Building Group, a registered building company based in Ballarat specialising in residential construction, including custom homes, rural builds and unit developments.

Combining practical building experience with a strong focus on innovation, Ash is also driving the adoption of 3D concrete printing in Australia through Dune Building Technologies, bringing emerging construction technology into real-world residential and commercial applications.

His work explores how new building methods can help address labour and skills shortages, construction efficiency, material waste, sustainability, durability and housing affordability.

Guest Speaker

Renee Briggs-Gordon

National Manager — Apprenticeships | APM

Renee Briggs-Gordon is National Manager — Apprenticeships at APM, where she leads the delivery of Australian Apprenticeship Support Services and works across government, industry and employers to strengthen apprenticeship outcomes.

A strategic leader with expertise in workforce development, compliance and contract management, Renee is focused on turning policy into practical solutions that improve apprentice retention, strengthen employer capability and create greater opportunities for underrepresented cohorts to participate and succeed.

Through her national role, Renee brings a broad perspective on the apprenticeship system and the importance of collaboration in creating sustainable pathways into skilled careers.

Guest Speaker

Julie Harbert

Team Leader — Mentoring and Personal Support Services | Apprenticeship Support Australia

Julie Harbert is Team Leader of Mentoring and Personal Support Services at Apprenticeship Support Australia, part of the Victorian Chamber, where she leads a team supporting apprentices, trainees, employers and industry stakeholders across Victoria.

With extensive experience across apprenticeships, workforce development, stakeholder engagement and learner support, Julie is passionate about improving apprenticeship commencement, retention, completion and wellbeing. Her work has included initiatives focused on early intervention, mentoring and creating more inclusive pathways for underrepresented cohorts, including women in trades, people with disability and First Nations apprentices.

Julie is a strong advocate for the role employers, supervisors and mentors play in shaping the apprentice experience. Drawing on frontline apprenticeship support and employer engagement, she brings valuable insight into the factors that influence whether apprentices feel supported, remain engaged and ultimately succeed.

People shaping the conversation

Panellist

Gary Workman

Executive Director | Apprenticeship Employment Network & GAN Australia

Gary Workman is Executive Director of the Apprenticeship Employment Network and Global Apprenticeship Network — Australia, bringing more than 20 years of experience across the Vocational Education and Training sector.

Since taking the helm of AEN in 2009, Gary has worked extensively across industry and government to strengthen apprenticeships, workforce development and employment pathways. AEN represents Victoria's not-for-profit Group Training Organisations, which collectively employ more than 5,000 apprentices and trainees across a broad network of host employers.

Gary is a strong advocate for quality apprenticeships and sustainable pathways that connect employers with the skilled workforce they need.

Panellist

Kirsty O'Connor

Construction Manager | Simonds Homes

Kirsty O'Connor is a Construction Manager with Simonds Homes and an experienced leader within Victoria's residential building industry. Having completed her Certificate IV and Diploma of Building and Construction, along with further management studies through Builders Academy Australia, Kirsty has continued to build her career through construction and leadership roles with Simonds Homes.

Named Builders Academy Australia's Overall 2021 Student of the Year and recognised among the 2025 Top 100 Women in Construction, Kirsty brings a valuable perspective on workforce development, career progression and the role industry plays in developing the next generation of skilled tradespeople and construction leaders.

Panellist

George Pougioukas

Head of Training | Builders Academy Australia & CWBTS

George Pougioukas is Head of Training across Builders Academy Australia and CWBTS, bringing more than 30 years of construction and vocational training experience to the role. With a career grounded in the building industry, George combines practical trade knowledge with a strong commitment to developing the next generation of skilled construction professionals.

Passionate about apprentice progression and retention, George believes commencing an apprenticeship is only the starting line. His focus is on strong connections between apprentices, employers and trainers, ensuring training reflects the realities of the job site and giving apprentices the support they need to progress through to completion.

Panellist

Nagy Mourad

Registered Builder · Trainer & Assessor | Beehive Homes Construction

Nagy Mourad is a Registered Builder, construction trainer and assessor, and founder of Beehive Homes Construction. With a background spanning engineering, senior operational leadership, property development and residential construction, Nagy brings a unique combination of technical, commercial and leadership experience to the building industry.

Alongside running his building business, Nagy teaches the Diploma of Building and Construction with Builders Academy Australia, supporting the next generation as they develop the skills and knowledge required to progress into supervisory, management and builder roles.

Nagy is passionate about bridging the gap between technical capability and the leadership, commercial understanding and professionalism required to lead people, projects and businesses.

Panellist

John Graves

Priority Apprenticeships Manager, Projects & Systems | Apprenticeships Victoria

John Graves is Priority Apprenticeships Manager, Projects & Systems at Apprenticeships Victoria, where he leads strategic initiatives designed to strengthen apprenticeship and traineeship outcomes across Victoria.

With extensive experience working across industry, government and the training sector, John has a strong understanding of both the opportunities and practical challenges associated with employing and developing apprentices and trainees. His work focuses on building partnerships and designing scalable approaches that support sustainable workforce development while delivering meaningful outcomes for employers, apprentices and government.

Prior to joining Apprenticeships Victoria, John was Program Manager — Major Projects at Holmesglen Institute, where he led engagement with some of Victoria's largest infrastructure projects, including the Metro Tunnel, North East Link and Suburban Rail Loop.

John brings a valuable system-wide perspective to The Builders Table, with experience connecting government priorities with the realities facing industry, employers and training providers. His focus on practical collaboration and stronger training and employment outcomes makes him an important voice in the conversation about how we collectively build Victoria's future skilled workforce.
